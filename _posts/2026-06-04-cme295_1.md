    - Precision

    - F1 score
- Generation

## 2. Tokenization
{: style="background-color:#ffeaa7; color:#000000; padding:4px 8px; border-radius:6px;" }

- <span style="color:#FF0000">**Tokenization**</span>: 텍스트를 토큰 단위로 쪼개는 과정

| Method | Pros | Cons |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Word-level** | Simple <br> Interpretable | Risk of OOV <br> Does not leverage knowledge of root |
| **Subword-level** <br><small>e.g. WordPiece, BPE</small> | Leverages common prefixes and suffixes <br> Learned from the data | Risk of OOV, though less than word-level |
| **Character-level** | Small chance of OOV <br> RoBUsT tO CASinG anD MIspeliNGs | Makes computations slower <br> Embeddings not interpretable |
